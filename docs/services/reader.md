# Reader services

[QM Reader](../worlds/qm-reader.md) gets its books from:

| Service | What it adds |
|---|---|
| **BookOrbit** | Library, audiobooks (with progress sync), requests by title, ISBN or author, star ratings, highlights, notes, reading stats |
| **Grimmory** | Library, series, shelves, read status. *New in 1.3* |
| **Hardcover** *(optional key)* | Trending books, charts, new releases |
