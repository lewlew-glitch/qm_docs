# Mac

On Apple silicon Macs, Quartermaster runs as the same app, with a layout made for the Mac:

- A **sidebar** to switch worlds, in place of the slider
- Layouts that fit the window, with wider grids
- In wide windows, Command Centre layouts use **two columns**
- The **Today** card sits in the spotlight
- **Keyboard shortcuts**
- Click outside a sheet to close it
