# Quartermaster

<p class="tagline">Your whole homelab, one app.</p>

Quartermaster is a native iPhone, iPad and Mac app for your self-hosted stack. It supports **64 services** across three worlds and talks straight to your servers. There are no accounts, no analytics and no cloud middleman.

## Why Quartermaster

- **Direct to your servers.** Once it's set up, the app talks to your services directly. Nothing goes through a relay.
- **Keys stay on your device.** API keys and passwords are encrypted on the device.
- **The Doctor.** When a download stalls, it explains the cause in plain English and offers a one-tap fix. [More](worlds/doctor.md)
- **Home and away.** Give each service a local and a remote address, and the app switches between them automatically. [More](getting-started/home-and-away.md)
- **One app on every Apple device.** It runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and Apple TV is in beta. [More](platforms/index.md)
