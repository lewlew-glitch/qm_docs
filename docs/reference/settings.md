# Settings

Open Settings from the **profile icon** at the top right of Home, then **Settings**.

## Connections & profiles

Services, profiles, shortcuts and Companion.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Set up your stack** | See what you've connected, and add more |
| **Hot Bar** | Shortcuts pinned to the top of Home. Turn it on or off |
| **Profiles** | Switch or manage your stacks. See [Profile credentials](../getting-started/home-and-away.md#profile-credentials) |
| **Services** | Turn connected services on or off |
| **QM Companion** | Your Companion connection. See [QM Companion](../companion/index.md) |
| **Install service updates** | Lets Quartermaster apply an update when you tap **Install** on a service that can update itself. Never automatic. Updates are shown either way |
| **Taste Profile** | What your library says about you |
| **Custom Links** | Bookmark your own dashboards and pages |
| **Saved Commands** | One-line SSH commands, ready on the terminal |

## Appearance & layout

Theme, Home, tabs, icons and one-handed use.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Theme** | Choose your accent colour |
| **App Icon** | Choose your compass finish |
| **Customise Home** | Choose what Home shows and where Trending comes from |
| **Customise Command Centre** | Layouts, the layout wizard, sections and machines |
| **Customise Tabs** | Choose the five tabs in each world's bottom bar |
| **One-handed corner button** | A floating button in Home's bottom corner that opens the top menu within reach of your thumb |
| **Scrolling text** | Long names that don't fit glide across after a moment. Turn it off to keep them still. Reduce Motion also keeps them still |

## Media & Reader

Metadata, playback and the QM Reader world.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **TMDB** | Your TMDB connection for metadata |
| **Trending now & browse: Source** | **Auto**, **Seerr** or **TMDB**. Auto uses Seerr while it's connected, otherwise TMDB. This source also feeds Home's browsing rows, catalogue search, Discover's new picks and the suggestions when you add a film or series |
| **Now Playing source** | **Auto**, **Plex** or **Tautulli** |
| **Open in Plex** | **Plex** app, **Infuse** or **Web**. Recent Plex versions can open on their home screen instead of the title, so pick **Web** if that happens |
| **Skip intros** | Jumps past an intro when your server has marked one, and VoiceOver says when it happens. Turn it off to watch intros. On a Mac, a **Skip Intro** button appears instead |
| **Play next episode** | As an episode ends, Up Next counts down 10 seconds, then plays the next one, up to three in a row. With this off, or with VoiceOver on, you choose each time |
| **Taste is based on** | **My library** (everything in Radarr and Sonarr, watched or not) or **What I actually watch** (read from Tautulli) |
| **Subtitles on title pages** | Shows the Subtitles card on film and episode pages, with Bazarr's tools or an offer to connect it |
| **QM Reader** | Turn the books world on or off. Off hides the world, its tabs and its screens |

## Notifications

Alerts, delivery and privacy controls. See [Alerts](../worlds/alerts.md).

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Push notifications** *(Pro)* | Apple notifications through Quartermaster's relay, even when the app is closed |
| **Instant alerts, your way** | Your services push straight to your own channel: ntfy, Pushover, Discord or Telegram |
| **Local alerts** | Foreground alerts, the daily digest and server thresholds, on this device |
| **From your Companion** *(Pro)* | Your own server sends the alerts, with the wording and rules you choose |

## General

Language, calendar and regional preferences.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Language** | The app's language, or follow the system |
| **Week starts on Monday** | Calendars start the week on Monday instead of Sunday |
| **Fahrenheit** | Show temperatures in °F. Some services, like Glances, report temperature in their own configured unit, and this doesn't change those |
| **Open the app on** | **Where you left off**, **Entertainment** or **Command Centre**. Takes effect the next time the app starts. Coming back from the background keeps you where you were |
