# Privacy and security

- **No accounts.** You never sign up or sign in to Quartermaster.
- **No tracking, analytics or telemetry.** Quartermaster is purely a client with no server behind it. Nothing about your stack is sent to us or anyone else.
- **No cloud middleman.** After setup, the app talks to your servers directly.
- **Keys stay on your device**, in the **iOS Keychain**, never in unencrypted app storage. Keys are also removed from all diagnostic logs.
- **The optional push relay** only stores your device's push token, never your keys, URLs or library data. See [Alerts](../worlds/alerts.md#push-notifications).
- **Demo mode** runs entirely on your device, with no network calls.
- **iCloud Sync** is protected by a passphrase you set. See [Sync and backups](sync-backups.md).
