# Stack handover

Stack handover lets a **fresh install** of Quartermaster take your whole service list from Companion, so you don't add services one by one. It uses the same [setup transfer](pairing.md#standard-setup-transfer) as the first time you paired.

This is useful for a new phone, a family member's iPad or a reset device.

## In Companion

1. Sign in and open **Set up**.
2. Choose the services to include, and check each one's **local address** and **away address**.
3. Create the transfer. Companion shows a **QR code** and, separately, a **setup code**. You can also download the transfer as a `.qmcompanion` file.

## In Quartermaster

1. Go to **Settings → Connections & profiles → QM Companion** and choose **Add services from Companion**.
2. Scan the QR code, or open the `.qmcompanion` file.
3. Enter the setup code. Quartermaster checks each service before it saves it.
4. **Check each service's address** before you finish.

The transfer **expires after three minutes** and works once. Importing is free. On the free plan one service is usable, and Pro unlocks the rest.
