# Entertainment

Entertainment covers your films, TV, music, books, photos and streaming. It connects to **34 services**, including Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, the *arr apps, downloaders, Seerr and stats tools.

Tabs: **Home · Movies · Shows · Discover · Search**

!!! note "Moved in 1.3"
    Stack, Stats, Downloads, Containers and the Doctor are now in [Command Centre](command-centre.md). **For You** can be added back as a tab.

## Home

Home opens on a full-screen **spotlight** with buttons to resume, play or open a title. Pull down to expand it.

Rows only appear when they have something to show. From top to bottom:

1. Spotlight
2. **Up Next**
3. **Arrived since yesterday**
4. **Just Added**, filterable by All / Films / Shows
5. **Not watched yet**
6. **Requests**
7. **Coming Soon**
8. Browsing shelves: *Trending now*, *Browse by genre*, *Top charts*, *New this month*, *Films out soon*

Browsing rows skip titles you already own. *Trending* and *Top charts* keep their real ranking and mark what's in your library.

### Customise Home

**Customise Home** turns each row on or off. Hidden rows load nothing. Trending and browsing rows can come from **Seerr** or **TMDB**.

On **Jellyfin** or **Emby**, you choose whose titles appear in *Up Next* and *Continue Watching*: one user's, or everyone's.

### Hot bar

You can also show the Command Centre [hot bar](command-centre.md#hot-bar) on Entertainment Home (iPhone and iPad).

## Watch it here

Play from **Jellyfin**, **Emby** and **Plex** in the app:

- Choose audio and subtitle tracks
- AirPlay and Picture in Picture
- Skips intros your server has marked
- **Up Next** counts down and plays up to three episodes in a row

## Family and friends

Invite people to browse your library and request titles through **Seerr**. You can send the invite as a **QR code**, a **link** or a **file**.

- Setting up sharing needs **Pro**.
- Joining is **free**.
