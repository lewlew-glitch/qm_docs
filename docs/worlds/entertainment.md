# Entertainment

Entertainment covers your films, TV, music, books, photos and streaming. It connects to **34 services**, including Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, the *arr apps, downloaders, Seerr and stats tools.

Default tabs: **Home · Movies · Shows · Discover · Search**. Choose your own five, such as adding **Calendar**, in **Settings → Appearance & layout → Customise Tabs**.

!!! note "Moved in 1.3"
    Stack, Stats, Downloads, Containers and the Doctor are now in [Command Centre](command-centre.md). **For You** can be added back with **Customise Tabs**.

## Home

Home opens on a full-screen **spotlight** with buttons to resume, play or open a title. Pull down to expand it. Swipe past the last title to go back to the first.

Tapping a card in Up Next or Continue Watching opens the film or episode. Press and hold for **Resume** or **Play**.

Rows only appear when they have something to show. From top to bottom:

1. Spotlight
2. **Continue Watching**, what you're partway through
3. **Up Next**, the next episodes of the shows you're watching, plus your Tonight picks
4. **Downloading**, while a film or episode is on its way, with progress and time left
5. **Arrived since yesterday**
6. **Just Added**, filterable by All / Films / Shows
7. **Not watched yet**
8. **Requests**
9. **Coming Soon**
10. Browsing shelves: *Trending now*, *Browse by genre*, *Top charts*, *New this month*, *Films out soon*

Browsing rows skip titles you already own, and follow Seerr's hide settings for titles you have, blocklisted titles and requested ones (requested needs Seerr 3.5+). *Trending* and *Top charts* keep their real ranking and mark what's in your library.

### Customise Home

**Customise Home** turns each row on or off, including Continue Watching, Up Next and Downloading separately. You'll find it, and **Customise Tabs**, at the bottom of Home. Hidden rows load nothing. Trending and browsing rows can come from **Seerr** or **TMDB**.

On **Jellyfin** or **Emby**, you choose whose titles appear in *Up Next* and *Continue Watching*: one user's, or everyone's.

### Hot bar

You can also show the Command Centre [hot bar](command-centre.md#hot-bar) on Entertainment Home (iPhone and iPad).

## Watch it here

Play from **Jellyfin**, **Emby** and **Plex** in the app:

- Choose audio and subtitle tracks
- AirPlay and Picture in Picture
- Skips intros your server has marked
- **Up Next** counts down and plays up to three episodes in a row

## Family and friends

Create a **Family & Friends** profile so people can browse your library and request titles through **Seerr**, while your settings stay behind your owner PIN. Hand them your device, or send the profile to theirs as a **QR code** or a **file**, protected by a passphrase.

- Setting up sharing needs **Pro**.
- Joining is **free**.

See [Share your library with family](../tutorials/family-sharing.md) for the full steps.
