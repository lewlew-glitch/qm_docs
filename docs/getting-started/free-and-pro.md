## If you don't buy Pro

Any extra services you added are **kept, not deleted**. One service and one profile stay usable. The rest show as locked, with their settings and keys saved until you unlock Pro.

## Pricing

Quartermaster is a free download. Pro is an in-app purchase:

| Option | US | UK |
|---|---|---|
| **Pro Monthly** | $4.99 / month | £4.99 / month |
| **Pro Annual** | $19.99 / year | £19.99 / year |
| **Quartermaster Pro** (lifetime, one-time) | $29.99 | £29.99 |

Every plan unlocks **exactly the same features**, and there are no separate tiers. Pick lifetime if you're staying long term, or monthly to try it out.

Prices are taken from the App Store listing and may differ in other regions. Apple shows the price for your region before you buy.

## Restore, cancel or refund

- **Restore Pro** on a new or reset device: go to **Settings → Restore purchase**, signed in with the same Apple ID.
- **Cancel the subscription:** go to **App Store → your Apple ID → Subscriptions → Quartermaster Pro → Cancel**.
- **Refunds** go through [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Continued Support

**Continued Support** ($9.99 / £9.99) is an optional purchase for anyone who wants to help cover the cost of developing Quartermaster. It doesn't unlock any extra features. Pro already includes everything.
